\section{Problem 1}\label{problem-1}

Are Metacritic reviews of the show expected to be at least 60\%
favorable or strongly favorable?

\section{Problem 2}\label{problem-2}

The observational unit is the Metacritic review of one episode.

\section{Problem 3}\label{problem-3}

Metacritic review; categorical (either favorable or strongly favorable,
or other rating)

\section{Problem 4}\label{problem-4}

Long run proportion of episodes that have a favorable or strongly
favorable Metacritic review (\(\pi\))

\section{Problem 5}\label{problem-5}

29/35 = 0.8285714

\section{Problem 6}\label{problem-6}

\begin{quote}
H0: The long run proportion of episodes that have a favorable or
strongly favorable Metacritic review is equal to 0.6. HA: The long run
proportion of episodes that have a favorable or strongly favorable
Metacritic review is greater than 0.6.
\end{quote}

\[
H_0:\pi=0.6
\]

\[
H_A: \pi\gt0.6
\]

\section{Problem 8}\label{problem-8}

Approximately 0.0030 (solutions will vary). This rejects the null
hypothesis since p-value \textless{} 0.05.

\section{Problem 9}\label{problem-9}

\(Z=\frac{\hat{p}-\pi}{SD}\approx 2.753873\). This is outside the 2's
and rejects the null hypothesis.

\section{Problem 10}\label{problem-10}

Conditions are not met for the theory-based approach since there are not
at least 10 successes and 10 failures.

\section{Problem 11}\label{problem-11}

There is evidence to conclude that the long run proportion of episodes
that have a favorable or strongly favorable Metacritic review is greater
than 0.6. They can produce the next season!
